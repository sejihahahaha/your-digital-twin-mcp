Are you a data-driven problem solver with a passion for optimising inventory and driving process improvements? We're seeking a talented Inventory and Data Analyst to join our team in Rydalmere.

Reporting to the Inventory Planning and Purchasing Manager, you'll be a key player in ensuring efficient inventory management and driving strategic improvements through data analysis and innovative projects.

Base: In-office role working 5 days a week at Rydalmere, NSW

Responsibilities:

Forecast & Optimise: Develop statistically based demand forecasts, incorporating market intelligence, and optimise inventory levels across AU & NZ.

Data Analysis & Reporting: Generate comprehensive reports on inventory levels, forecasting accuracy, and supply chain performance for management.

Project Leadership: Spearhead projects to improve processes through automation, modelling, AI, and other cutting-edge solutions.

Inventory Management: Manage SLOB reviews, conduct ABC classification, and analyse underperforming products to drive optimisation.

ERP & Dashboards: Ensure timely and accurate data uploads within the ERP system and maintain accurate user dashboards and reports.

S&OP Support: Refine and maintain the demand planning discipline for the monthly S&OP cycle.

Data Health & Collaboration: Conduct master data health checks and collaborate across departments to ensure operational continuity.

Training & Mentoring: Train and mentor team members within the Waterco Group (inclusive of Davey Water)

About you & what you'll bring:

Demonstrated capability and experience in Inventory, Logistics, and Data Analytics.

Degree or equivalent in Supply Chain, Logistics Management, or Data Analytics.

Great communicator with flexible interpersonal skills to manage a diverse range of stakeholders

Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Power BI, PowerPoint, Visio), and SQL.

Strong analytical, numerical, and communication skills.

Self-motivated, highly organized, and detail-oriented.
